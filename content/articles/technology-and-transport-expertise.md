## Technology meets operational experience

In 2025, FLOWS reported on transport entrepreneur Kurt Joosen joining Inuits. The story brings together knowledge of transport operations and the ability to develop digital solutions.

For readers interested in entrepreneurship and logistics, it is a practical example of connecting complementary experience around the challenges of an industry.

## Explore the background

Inuits grew from a small Antwerp technology venture into an international IT services business. Its open-source expertise and industry partnerships form part of Kris Kerremans' entrepreneurial background.
