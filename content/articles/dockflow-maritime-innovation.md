## Supporting a logistics business as it grows

In March 2022, Bloovi reported that Kris Kerremans took a minority stake in Dockflow. The article did not disclose the investment amount.

The announcement described plans to expand the customer base, develop features for maritime logistics and grow the team.

## Visibility across container transport

Dockflow develops technology that helps logistics professionals follow shipments and coordinate information. The story connects a practical operational challenge with the technology and experience needed to build a business around it.

This summary reflects the historical announcement, rather than a statement about the company's current ownership or performance.
