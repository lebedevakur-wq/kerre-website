## Backing practical business software

Trends reported in August 2022 that Kris Kerremans invested €1 million in Striktly, becoming a minority shareholder.

Striktly develops software that connects customer relationship management, enterprise resource planning and pre-accounting. The investment supported the company's ambition to develop and scale its offering.

## More than financial support

The report also highlighted the contribution of IT expertise and an international network alongside the investment: experience that could help the team develop its software and technology.

This is a historical announcement. The original report provides the context at the time of publication.
