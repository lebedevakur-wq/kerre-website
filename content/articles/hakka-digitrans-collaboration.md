## Bringing AI expertise into logistics

In March 2021, imec announced Hakka's participation in DigiTrans, a spin-off of the University of Antwerp and imec. The companies had agreed to work more closely together in February of that year.

The collaboration connected Hakka's container logistics platform with DigiTrans' AI expertise in data interpretation and planning.

## Less repetitive work for transport planners

One application was DigiTrans' Data Entry Assistant, designed to turn unstructured transport information from documents and emails into standardised data for business systems.

For logistics operators, the practical aim was to reduce manual entry and make it easier to coordinate transport information. The original announcement explains the collaboration and its intended applications.
