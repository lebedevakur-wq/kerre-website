Kris Kerremans.

An entrepreneur and private investor, Kris Kerremans brings experience in technology and logistics to Kerre. His work today centres on supporting entrepreneurs and working alongside co-investors.

Built in Antwerp.
Shaped by experience.

Kris’s father worked as a ship repairer. Childhood drives through the Port of Antwerp sparked a lasting fascination with the people and processes that kept maritime logistics moving.

    Co-founding Inuits

    Kris and four fellow IT professionals co-found Inuits in a small attic in Antwerp. The venture grows into an international IT services business built on open-source expertise, digital infrastructure and close customer relationships.

  2. Logistics

    Connecting transport companies

    Hakka brings technology into transport collaboration. Building on the Eurotranscom concept, it helps container transport companies share information, coordinate assignments and reduce empty journeys.

    Bringing AI into logistics

    Hakka takes a participation in DigiTrans, connecting transport collaboration with AI expertise in data processing and planning.
