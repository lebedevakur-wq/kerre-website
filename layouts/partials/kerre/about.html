Built in Antwerp.
Shaped by experience.

My father’s work in ship repair sparked an early interest in maritime logistics. Co-founding Inuits in Antwerp in 2007 brought technology, entrepreneurship and industry experience together.

Read the entrepreneurial background

My connection to maritime logistics began long before I started a company. My father worked as a ship repairer, and childhood drives through the Port of Antwerp sparked a fascination with the people and processes that kept it moving.

In 2007, four fellow IT professionals and I co-founded Inuits in a small attic in Antwerp. An independent technology venture grew into an international IT services business, built around open-source expertise, digital infrastructure and close customer relationships.

That experience eventually brought technology and logistics together. Projects such as Hakka showed me how sharing information and connecting companies could help solve practical transport challenges.

Read about the move into investing

As I became involved in other technology ventures, investing felt like a natural extension of entrepreneurship: an opportunity to support founders with experience, capital and access to a network.

I know what it takes to develop products, assemble teams and navigate uncertainty. I want to contribute useful introductions, technological understanding and a constructive perspective, while respecting the founder's leadership.

Read the investment perspective

My interests sit where technology meets business operations: enterprise software, digital transformation, AI, digital infrastructure and maritime logistics.

I look for people with integrity, ambition and resilience, and for technology that solves real problems. Trust, collaboration and a sound business model matter as much as the idea itself.

The motivation remains the same: helping entrepreneurs turn important ideas into lasting businesses.
