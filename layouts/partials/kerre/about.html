About Kris

Entrepreneur by experience. Investor by conviction.

Belgian entrepreneur and angel investor with a passion for technology, meaningful innovation and businesses that make a difference.

The short version

A career built on curiosity and collaboration.

In 2007, I co-founded Inuits with four fellow IT professionals in a small attic in Antwerp.

What started as an independent technology venture grew into an international IT services business, built around open-source expertise, digital infrastructure and close customer relationships.

Along the way, I found opportunities to combine technology with a lifelong fascination for maritime logistics. Today, my experience as a founder shapes how I support ambitious entrepreneurs and invest in practical innovation.

Beyond capital

I approach investing as someone who knows what it takes to build a business—from developing products and assembling teams to navigating uncertainty. I want to contribute expertise, relevant connections and a constructive perspective.
