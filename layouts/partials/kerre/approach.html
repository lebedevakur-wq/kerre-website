My Approach

Investing with an entrepreneurial mindset.

Great businesses begin with people who see a better way forward. I look for determination, practical innovation and the ability to create long-term value.

Guiding principles
01 /

People before ideas

I look for founders who understand their market, believe in their product and remain open to learning. Trust and transparency are at the core of every good partnership.

02 /

Technology with purpose

The most compelling innovations solve real problems. I value products that improve efficiency, reduce complexity or give businesses entirely new capabilities.

03 /

Collaboration creates opportunity

By connecting people, businesses and data in thoughtful ways, technology can achieve more than any individual organisation could alone.

04 /

Build for the long term

Meaningful growth takes patience, a strong team and a sound business model. A sustainable foundation matters more than short-lived excitement.

05 /

Beyond the cheque

I aim to bring firsthand experience, technological understanding, practical advice and useful introductions—while respecting the founder's leadership.
