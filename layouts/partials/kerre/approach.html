The Kerre approach

More than capital.
A shared passion to build.

Backing entrepreneurs takes more than belief in an idea. It takes trust, practical experience and a shared commitment to building a business that lasts.

What matters in a partnership

01 /

Ambitious People

Entrepreneurs with a clear vision, the ability to execute and the determination to keep moving forward.

Understanding the market matters. So do integrity, resilience and the willingness to listen and learn. Good partnerships begin with trust and honest conversations.

02 /

Meaningful Businesses

Companies solving real problems through scalable technology or a defensible business model.

The opportunity should be clear: improve how something works, reduce complexity or give customers a useful new capability. Lasting value needs a sound foundation and the patience to build it.

03 /

Active Partnership

Strategic thinking, operational experience and relevant connections alongside financial capital.

Kris brings a founder's perspective on technology, teams and business development. The aim is to be a constructive partner: sharing practical advice, making useful introductions and respecting the entrepreneur's leadership.

Great companies are often built with the support of complementary investors. Kerre is open to working alongside co-investors whose interests, values and expertise align, bringing relevant insight, networks and a shared ambition to build long-term value.
